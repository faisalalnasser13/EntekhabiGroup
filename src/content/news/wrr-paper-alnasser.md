---
title: "Faisal AlNasser paper accepted to Water Resources Research"
date: 2026-06-08
---

Congratulations to PhD student Faisal AlNasser — his manuscript on integrating satellite soil moisture retrievals with land surface models has been accepted for publication in *Water Resources Research*. The work develops a framework for reconciling coarse-scale remote sensing observations with process-based hydrologic simulations, with applications to drought monitoring and water balance closure at the catchment scale.
