Research

Water Cycle & Land-Atmosphere Interactions

We study how water moves through the land surface and atmosphere using satellite observations, field measurements, and models. Current projects include soil moisture retrieval, drought monitoring, and coupling hydrologic models with remote sensing data.

Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

We develop methods to extract geophysical variables from satellite imagery at regional and global scales. Applications include crop mapping, land cover change detection, and training data for Earth observation foundation models.

Data Assimilation & Forecasting

We integrate remote sensing observations into predictive models to improve forecasts of hydrologic and atmospheric variables. This includes ensemble Kalman filtering, machine learning emulators, and uncertainty quantification.
